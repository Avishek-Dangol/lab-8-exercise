1. Move mTransform out. Position and scale are needed by lots of things that are not sprites, 
like a hitbox or a camera, while the texture and vertex data are only there for drawing.

2. Draw the see-through sprites from back to front, so the farther one is drawn first. 
The blend depends on which sprite is drawn on top of which, so stacked tinted windows only look right if the back window is already drawn before the front one.
